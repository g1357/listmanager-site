List Manager Privacy Policy

Revision: November 6, 2026.

Developer and scope

Developer: ИП Дукаревич Г.Б. (individual entrepreneur). Privacy questions and support: ipgbd@yandex.ru.

This policy covers the offline Android editions of List Manager identified as com.gbd.listmanager (RuStore) and com.gbd.listmanager.demo (Google Play). These are separate installations with separate internal storage; the app does not automatically exchange data between them. No account is required to use lists.

Data on your device

The app processes list and item names, descriptions, notes, ordering, and completion and deletion flags that you enter. JSON documents also contain local object identifiers and technical format and save-time information. These identifiers support editing records and are not used to track you.

Documents, previously saved versions (.bak), temporary files and diagnostic copies are stored in the app's internal directory. Preferences include the active document name, display settings and a version-notice marker. Errors may produce local startup-errors.log and import-errors.log files with technical details; these may contain paths and data fragments related to the error.

The app uses this information to display, edit, save and recover lists. It does not automatically send list content, preferences or logs to the developer. It has no advertising, analytics SDKs, registration or developer-operated synchronization server. Core features work offline.

File selection and system backup

When importing, you select a document using the system file picker. The app reads and validates the selected file and creates an internal copy. It does not modify or delete the external original. Your selected file provider, including a cloud provider, may transfer data under its own terms.

Android system backup is enabled. Depending on your device, settings and provider, the operating system may copy documents, preferences, backup files and diagnostic files to system backup storage and transfer them to another device. This is not a List Manager cloud service; the developer does not gain access to that storage through the app. The provider determines its retention, transfer, deletion and size limits. Complete backup and recovery are not guaranteed. Manage system copies in Android settings and the relevant service.

Support emails

If you independently email ipgbd@yandex.ru, the developer receives your sender address, message content and any materials you voluntarily attach. This information is used to respond and resolve your request, not for advertising or profiling. Logs and screenshots are not requested for feedback; please do not send passwords or confidential list content.

Correspondence passes through email providers, including Yandex for the support address, and is processed under their terms. The developer retains correspondence for as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations, then deletes information no longer needed. You may request deletion of correspondence at the support address; if some information must be retained to meet obligations, the response will explain this.

Retention and deletion

Local documents and diagnostic files remain until deleted or the app's storage is cleared using device controls; there is no automatic log expiry period. A Deleted flag or hiding entries does not physically erase them from JSON, .bak or other copies. A .bak file holds the previously saved version, which may not contain your latest changes.

Clearing app data or uninstalling may cause loss of local lists. First preserve any data you need using a method available to you. These actions do not remove external originals, emails or system-provider copies. System restoration may bring back previously saved data. The developer cannot remotely delete your local documents. There is no List Manager account to delete.

Protection and third-party services

The app uses internal app storage and Android access controls. It does not provide separate JSON encryption or document passwords; it is not a secure vault for secrets. Protect your device using operating-system controls.

The app store, Android, email providers and file providers may independently process data when you use their services. Their own privacy policies govern that processing; offline functionality in List Manager does not mean these services never use a network.

Policy webpage

When hosted on GitHub Pages, this page is served by GitHub. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security under its privacy statement. The page itself has no analytics scripts, advertising tools, forms or external fonts. Opening it in a browser does not send the contents of your lists to the developer.

Changes and contact

This policy is updated when features or data practices change; the revision date is shown above. For questions about data or this policy, contact ИП Дукаревич Г.Б.: ipgbd@yandex.ru.
